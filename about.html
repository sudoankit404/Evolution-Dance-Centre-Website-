OUR STORY

Evolution Dance Centre was founded with a vision to create a space where dance enthusiasts could explore, learn, and master various dance forms under expert guidance. Located in the heart of Uttam Nagar, New Delhi, we have become a premier destination for dance education.

Our academy is affiliated with the prestigious Suro Bharti Sangeet Kala Kendra, ensuring that our students receive certified training that is recognized and respected across India. We take pride in maintaining the highest standards of dance education.

From street styles like Hip Hop and Breaking to traditional forms like Classical and Semi-Classical, we offer comprehensive training in 9 different dance styles. Our experienced instructors bring years of performance and teaching expertise to every class.
